Michael Yefet (the "Business") respects your privacy. This policy describes how information is collected and used in the Talmify App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Information voluntarily provided by the User: When contacting us via the "Contact Us" form, we collect your email address, the subject of the inquiry, and the content of the message.
Clarification: Please avoid sending medical, sensitive, or private information within the free-text message.

1.2. Information collected automatically (Analytics): We use third-party services (such as Google Analytics for Firebase) to collect anonymous statistical information about App usage (device type, crashes, usage duration) for service improvement purposes.

1.3. Purchase Information: We do not store credit card details. Information regarding your subscription status (active/inactive), purchase history, and subscription type is received and processed to unlock access to premium features.

2. Use of Information and Sharing with Third Parties

The Business does not sell personal information to third parties. The information is used by us to provide the Service, technical support, and to improve the App.
We share information with external service providers for the operation of the App:
* Google Firebase: For data storage, statistical analysis, and receiving data from other providers.
* Apple Inc.: For payment processing, subscription management, iCloud backup (if enabled by you), and provision of Apple Intelligence services.
* RevenueCat: A platform for subscription infrastructure management. This service is used for receipt validation, managing your subscription status, and synchronizing purchase data with analytics services (Firebase) to improve user experience.
* TikTok: We utilize the TikTok platform to measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns and for install attribution. The information transmitted to this service is strictly technical (such as IP address and basic device data upon launching the App). We hereby clarify that we do not conduct, request permission for, or permit tracking of users across third-party applications or websites (Cross-App Tracking). The information is used solely for the Business's internal statistical purposes, and its processing by this third party is subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Services

When using AI-based features, information (texts you entered) may be processed on your device or sent for processing on the servers of AI providers (such as Apple), in accordance with the privacy policies of those providers.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect the information. However, it is impossible to guarantee absolute security against unauthorized intrusions.

5. User Rights

You are entitled to review the personal information held about you (Contact Us inquiries) and request its correction or deletion, subject to applicable law, by contacting: legal@talmify.com.

6. Changes to Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the App after the update constitutes agreement to the new policy.
